Questions, answered

Still unsure? Talk to a real person.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Latchly runs in the browser. Add the optional app on your phone for push approvals.

What if I lose my phone?

Use your saved recovery codes or another trusted device, then sign out the lost one from your dashboard.

Can my team use single sign-on?

Yes. The Team plan connects to the identity tools you already use.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Personal is free for one person, with passkeys and alerts included.
